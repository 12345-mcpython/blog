强化后：After Kafka's teammate uses an attack on an enemy target, Kafka immediately launches Follow-Up ATK and deals Lightning DMG equal to 140% of Kafka's ATK to the primary target, with a 100% base chance to inflict Shock (equivalent to that applied by her Ultimate) on the attacked enemy target for 2 turns. This effect can trigger up to 2 time(s), 1 of which can be regained at the end of Kafka's turn.
