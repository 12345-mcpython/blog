- 破韧值: 单体 10, 全体 0, 扩散 0

Aventurine additionally gains **1** point(s) of "Blind Bet" after getting attacked. Upon reaching 7 points of "Blind Bet," Aventurine consumes the 7 points to launch a **7**-hit Follow-Up ATK, with each hit dealing Imaginary DMG equal to **25%** of Aventurine's DEF to one random enemy. "Blind Bet" is capped at 10 points.*
