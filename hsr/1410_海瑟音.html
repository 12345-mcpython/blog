终结技：绝海回涛，噬魂舞曲 / Maelstrom Rhapsody

  • 攻击类型: 终结技 · 效果: 全体攻击 · 元素: 物理 · 最高等级: 15
  • 破韧值: 单体 0, 全体 60, 扩散 0
  • 释放所需能量: 110

海瑟音展开结界，使敌方目标攻击力降低15.00%，防御力降低25.00%，对敌方全体造成等同于海瑟音200%攻击力的物理属性伤害。
处于结界中的敌方目标每受到1次持续伤害，海瑟音对其造成等同于自身80.00%攻击力的物理属性持续伤害，该伤害在每回合开始时或我方目标单次攻击后触发，且最多触发8次。该伤害不会重复触发此效果。
结界持续3回合，自身每回合开始时结界持续回合数减1。当海瑟音陷入无法战斗状态时，结界也会被解除。

Hysilens deploys a Zone that reduces enemy targets' ATK by 15.00% and DEF by 25.00%, and deals Physical DMG equal to 200% of Hysilens's ATK to all enemies.
For every 1 instance of DoT taken by an enemy target within the Zone, Hysilens deals Physical DoT equal to 80.00% of her ATK to them. This damage triggers at the start of each turn or after one attack by an ally target, up to 8 time(s). And it cannot repeatedly trigger this effect.
The Zone lasts for 3 turn(s) and this duration decreases by 1 at the start of this unit's every turn. When Hysilens gets knocked down, the Zone will also be dispelled.

  • 无法战斗状态：当我方目标的当前生命值被削减至0时进入的特殊状态，处于该状态的目标无法继续战斗。

  • 攻击类型: 无 · 效果: 削弱 · 元素: 无 · 最高等级: 15
  • 破韧值: 单体 0, 全体 0, 扩散 0

风化/灼烧/触电状态下，敌方目标每回合开始时受到等同于海瑟音25.00%攻击力的风/火/雷属性的持续伤害，持续2回合。

When an ally target attacks, there is a 100% base chance for Hysilens to inflict the hit enemy target with one of the following states: Wind Shear, Bleed, Burn, or Shock. Priority is given to inflicting a different state.
While in the Wind Shear/Burn/Shock state, at the start of each turn, the enemy target takes Wind/Fire/Lightning DoT equal to 25.00% of Hysilens's ATK for 2 turn(s).
While in the Bleed state, at the start of each turn, the enemy target takes Physical DoT equal to 20.00% of their Max HP, up to 25.00% of Hysilens's ATK, lasting for 2 turn(s).
