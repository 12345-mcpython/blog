  • 破韧值: 单体 10, 全体 0, 扩散 0

For any single ally with "Fortified Wager," their Effect RES increases by 50.00%, and when they get attacked, Aventurine gains 1 point of "Blind Bet." When Aventurine has "Fortified Wager," he can resist Crowd Control debuffs. This effect can trigger again after 2 turn(s). Aventurine additionally gains 1 point(s) of "Blind Bet" after getting attacked. Upon reaching 7 points of "Blind Bet," Aventurine consumes the 7 points to launch a 7-hit Follow-Up ATK, with each hit dealing Imaginary DMG equal to 25% of Aventurine's DEF to one random enemy. "Blind Bet" is capped at 10 points.
