### 战技：升变，士皆可帅 / Pawn's Promotion

- 攻击类型: 战技 · 效果: `辅助` · 元素: `无` · 最高等级: 15
- 破韧值: 单体 0, 全体 0, 扩散 0

>
> *Grants "Military Merit" to one designated ally character and gives Cerydra <strong>1</strong> points of Charge. Charge is capped at <strong>8</strong> points. When Charge reaches <strong>6</strong> points, automatically upgrades the character's "Military Merit" to "Peerage" and dispels their <u>Crowd Control debuffs</u>. The character with "Peerage" is considered to have "Military Merit" simultaneously. The character with "Peerage" increases the CRIT DMG for their dealt Skill DMG by <strong>72%</strong>, increases their All-Type <u>RES PEN</u> by <strong>10.00%</strong>, and triggers <u>Coup de Main</u> when using their Skill on enemy targets. After <u>Coup de Main</u> ends, consumes <strong>6</strong> points of Charge to revert "Peerage" to "Military Merit."*

- **控制类负面状态**：冻结，纠缠，禁锢，支配，怒噪，强烈震荡，异梦，缠禁，恐惧，行动锁定，幸福傀儡，怨火灼身。
- **抗性穿透**：当造成伤害时，无视敌方目标的一部分对应伤害属性的抗性值。
- **奇袭**：复制一次即将施放的技能并提前施放，随后施放原技能。<br>奇袭不会再次触发奇袭。
