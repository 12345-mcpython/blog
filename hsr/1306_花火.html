When Sparkle uses this ability on herself, the Action Advance effect will not trigger.

终结技：一人千役 / The Hero with a Thousand Faces

  • 攻击类型: 终结技 · 效果: 辅助 · 元素: 无 · 最高等级: 15
  • 破韧值: 单体 0, 全体 0, 扩散 0
  • 释放所需能量: 110

强化前：为我方恢复4个战技点，并使我方全体获得【谜诡】。持有【谜诡】的我方目标触发花火天赋造成的伤害提高效果每层额外提高10.00%，持续2回合。

强化后：为我方恢复6个战技点，若恢复时战技点溢出，则记录溢出的战技点数，最多记录10点。我方角色回合结束后若战技点小于上限，花火消耗记录值为我方恢复战技点，直至恢复至上限。随后使我方全体获得【谜诡】。持有【谜诡】的我方目标触发花火天赋造成的敌方受到伤害提高效果每层额外提高6.00%，持续3回合。

强化前：Recovers 4 Skill Points for allies and grants all allies "Cipher." For ally targets with "Cipher," each stack of the DMG Boost effect provided by Sparkle's Talent additionally increases by 10.00%, lasting for 2 turns.

强化后：Recovers 6 Skill Points for allies. If Skill Points overflow during recovery, the excess points are recorded, up to 10 points. When an ally character's turn ends, if Skill Points are below the maximum, Sparkle consumes the recorded value to recover Skill Points until the upper limit is reached. Then, grants all allies "Cipher." For ally targets with "Cipher," each stack of Boost of DMG taken by enemies provided by Sparkle's Talent additionally increases by 6.00%, lasting for 3 turn(s).
