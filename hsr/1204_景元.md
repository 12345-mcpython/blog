- 破韧值: 单体 15, 全体 0, 扩散 0

> 战斗开始时召唤【神君】。【神君】初始拥有<strong>60</strong>点速度以及<strong>3</strong>段攻击段数，行动时发动<u>追加攻击</u>，每段攻击对随机敌方单体造成等同于景元攻击力<strong>66%</strong>的雷属性伤害，同时对其相邻目标造成等同于主目标<strong>25%</strong>的雷属性伤害。<br>【神君】最多累计<strong>10</strong>段攻击段数且每增加1段攻击段数，速度提高<strong>10</strong>点，行动结束后速度和攻击段数恢复至初始状态。<br>当景元陷入<u>无法战斗状态</u>时【神君】消失。<br>当景元受到<u>控制类负面状态</u>影响时【神君】也无法行动。
>
> *Summons "Lightning-Lord" at the start of the battle. "Lightning-Lord" has <strong>60</strong> base SPD and <strong>3</strong> base Hits Per Action. When the Lightning-Lord takes action, its hits are considered as <u>Follow-Up ATKs</u>, with each hit dealing Lightning DMG equal to <strong>66%</strong> of Jing Yuan's ATK to a random single enemy, and enemies adjacent to it also receive Lightning DMG equal to <strong>25%</strong> of the DMG dealt to the primary target enemy.<br>The Lightning-Lord's Hits Per Action can reach a max of <strong>10</strong>. Every time "Lightning-Lord's" Hits Per Action increases by 1, its SPD increases by <strong>10</strong>. After the "Lightning-Lord's" action ends, its SPD and Hits Per Action return to their base values.<br>When Jing Yuan is <u>knocked down</u>, the "Lightning-Lord" will disappear.<br>When Jing Yuan is affected by <u>Crowd Control debuff</u>, the "Lightning-Lord" is unable to take action.*

- **追加攻击**：满足条件时自动触发的效果，对目标施放1次额外的攻击。
- **无法战斗状态**：当我方目标的当前生命值被削减至0时进入的特殊状态，处于该状态的目标无法继续战斗。
- **控制类负面状态**：冻结，纠缠，禁锢，支配，怒噪，强烈震荡，异梦，缠禁，恐惧，行动锁定，幸福傀儡，怨火灼身。
