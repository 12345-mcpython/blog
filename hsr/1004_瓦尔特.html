强化后：对敌方全体造成等同于瓦尔特150%攻击力的虚数属性伤害，有100%的基础概率使受到攻击的敌方目标陷入禁锢状态，持续1回合。
禁锢状态下，敌方目标行动延后12.00%，速度降低10%。施放终结技后使敌方全体陷入【失重】状态，【失重】状态下的敌方目标受到攻击时行动延后4.00%，该效果每个目标每回合内最多触发8次，【失重】状态持续2回合。

强化前：Deals Imaginary DMG equal to 150% of Welt's ATK to all enemies, with a 100% base chance for enemies hit by this ability to be Imprisoned for 1 turn.
Imprisoned enemies have their actions delayed by 40.00% and SPD reduced by 10%.

强化后：Deals Imaginary DMG equal to 150% of Welt's ATK to all enemies. Has a 100% base chance to Imprison hit enemy targets for 1 turn.
While Imprisoned, enemy targets have their actions delayed by 12.00% and their SPD reduced by 10%. After using his Ultimate, inflicts "Weightless" on all enemies. When targets in "Weightless" state get attacked, their actions are delayed by 4.00%. This effect can trigger up to 8 time(s) per target per turn. "Weightless" lasts for 2 turn(s).
