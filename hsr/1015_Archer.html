战技：伪•螺旋剑 / Caladbolg II: Fake Spiral Sword

  • 攻击类型: 战技 · 效果: 单体攻击 · 元素: 量子 · 最高等级: 15
  • 破韧值: 单体 60, 全体 0, 扩散 0

进入【回路连接】状态。对指定敌方单体造成等同于Archer360%攻击力的量子属性伤害。【回路连接】状态下施放战技后，本回合不会结束，并使Archer战技造成的伤害提高100%，该效果可以叠加2层，持续至退出【回路连接】状态。主动施放5次战技后或战技点不足以再次施放战技则退出【回路连接】状态。每个波次中的所有敌方目标被消灭后会退出【回路连接】状态。

Enters the "Circuit Connection" state. Deals Quantum DMG equal to 360% of Archer's ATK to one designated enemy. After using Skill in the "Circuit Connection" state, the current turn does not end, and the DMG dealt by Archer's Skill increases by 100%. This effect can stack up to 2 time(s), lasting until he exits the "Circuit Connection" state. After actively using Skill 5 time(s) or when Skill Points are insufficient to use Skill again, exits the "Circuit Connection" state. After all enemy targets have been defeated in each wave, exits the "Circuit Connection" state.

天赋：心眼（真） / Mind's Eye (True)

  • 攻击类型: 无 · 效果: 单体攻击 · 元素: 量子 · 最高等级: 15
  • 破韧值: 单体 30, 全体 0, 扩散 0

当Archer的队友对敌方目标施放攻击后，Archer消耗1点充能，立即对主目标发动追加攻击，造成等同于Archer200%攻击力的量子属性伤害，并恢复1个战技点。若本次追加攻击施放前目标被消灭则对敌方随机单体发动追加攻击。

After Archer's teammates attack enemy targets, Archer consumes 1 Charge and immediately launches Follow-Up ATK on the primary target, dealing Quantum DMG equal to 200% of Archer's ATK and recovering 1 Skill Point. If the target is defeated before this Follow-Up ATK is launched, the Follow-Up ATK will be directed at one random enemy instead.

  • 追加攻击：满足条件时自动触发的效果，对目标施放1次额外的攻击。
